# Millbrook Herald house style

## Numbers

- Use figures for 10 and above, words for one to nine.
- Money: `£420,000`, `£4.2m`. Never `£4.2 million` or `4.2m pounds`.
- Percentages: use the `%` sign, as in `65%`. Never write `per cent` or `percent`.

## Dates

- Write `the tenth`, never `November 10th` or `10/11`.

## Titles

- First mention: full title and name, as in `Councillor Ranasinghe`. After that, surname only.
- The mayor is `the mayor`, lower case, unless it comes before the name: `Mayor Okafor`.

## Banned words

These words take sides or overstate. Replace them with a plain verb.

| Banned  | Use            |
| ------- | -------------- |
| slammed | criticised     |
| blasted | criticised     |
| chaos   | disruption     |
| shock   | (leave it out) |
