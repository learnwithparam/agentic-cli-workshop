New walk-in clinic for Millbrook

A walk-in clinic will open at the old post office on Bridge Street, the health partnership will announce.
It will see up to 120 patients a day and open seven mornings a week.
Dr Amara Osei, clinical lead, said: "People should not have to travel to the city for a minor injury."
