MILLBROOK COUNCIL PRESS OFFICE
For immediate release

Millbrook Library to open longer

Millbrook Library will open until 8pm on weekday evenings from the first, the council said.
The change costs £46,000 a year and was approved in the council budget.
Councillor Priya Natt, cabinet member for culture, said: "Evening hours mean people who work can use the library too."
The library currently closes at 6pm.
