MILLBROOK COUNCIL PRESS OFFICE
For immediate release

No rise in market stall fees

Stall fees at Millbrook market will stay the same next year, the markets committee agreed.
Councillor Dev Ranasinghe, chair of the markets committee, said: "We will not raise stall fees this year. Traders have had a hard enough time."
Mayor Helen Okafor welcomed the decision.
The market hall roof repair, costing £92,000, was not approved and will be looked at again in the spring.
