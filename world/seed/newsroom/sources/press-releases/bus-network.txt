MILLBROOK COUNCIL PRESS OFFICE
For immediate release

Changes to two bus routes

Routes 12 and 41 will run every 30 minutes instead of every 20 minutes from January, the council said.
The council will spend £610,000 supporting the bus network next year.
Councillor Tom Barker, cabinet member for transport, said: "We have kept every route running, which many councils could not."
Passenger groups said the change would make journeys to the hospital harder.
