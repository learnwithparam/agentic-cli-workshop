MILLBROOK COUNCIL PRESS OFFICE
For immediate release

Leisure pool reopens after refit

Millbrook Leisure Pool will reopen on 8 December after a refit.
The refit cost £1,380,000, which is 15% more than the £1,200,000 first planned, because of a delay in parts.
Councillor Priya Natt said: "The pool will be warmer, brighter and open longer."
