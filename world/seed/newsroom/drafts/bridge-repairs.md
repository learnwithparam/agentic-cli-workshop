Millbrook Council will spend £4.2m repairing the Mill Lane bridge, which has been closed to lorries since the spring.

The work is part of the highways budget approved by councillors. Station Road will also be resurfaced at a cost of £185,000.

Drivers will be sent along Fenwick Road while the bridge is closed.
