# Desk rules for my own folder

## What this folder is

My notes and recipes for home. I read the trip plan before each trip, and I cook from the recipes on the weekend.

## Check before filing

- Every date in `notes/trip-plan.md` must match the dates in the ferry tickets I name.
- Every amount in `recipes/lentil-soup.md` and `recipes/pancakes.md` must use one unit.
- A budget in `notes/trip-plan.md` must add up to the total it states.

## Never do

- Never delete or rename a file. Say what you would change instead.
- Never write outside this folder.
